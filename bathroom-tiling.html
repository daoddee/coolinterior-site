Your questions, answered.

Can I book bathroom tilers near me?

Send your London postcode, photographs and approximate wall and floor measurements. We will confirm coverage and availability, discuss your tile choice and explain what is needed for an accurate quotation.

How much does bathroom tiling cost in London?

A useful quote needs the wall and floor areas, tile dimensions, existing surface condition, removal requirements and details of recesses or trims. Send photographs and measurements; we do not publish a fixed per-room price without reviewing the work.

Can new tiles go over old bathroom tiles?

That depends on whether the existing finish and background are sound, suitable and compatible with the proposed installation. Extra thickness can affect fittings, doors and edges. The background needs assessment before this is agreed.

How long will the work take?

Timing depends on the area, preparation, tile format, cuts and product curing requirements. Share the scope so we can discuss a realistic sequence and availability before work starts.

Bathroom tiles that balance comfort, care and design.

Clear information helps you choose a finish that suits your home. These are starting points for a project conversation; the final specification and schedule follow assessment of your space.

What works best in a bathroom?

Glazed ceramic can be a useful wall option; floor-rated porcelain can suit a coordinated wall-and-floor scheme. Choose each product for its intended location. A polished surface that looks attractive on a wall may be inappropriate on a wet floor. We discuss samples, cleaning, grout joints and any slip-resistance information with your household’s needs in mind.

Who installs the different parts?

Cool Interior handles the agreed tiling scope. Before starting, we clarify responsibility for background repairs, waterproofing, plumbing and electrical work. Tiling must be sequenced around the bath, shower fittings and drainage design. Any specialist or separately appointed trade needs to work to the same agreed plan.

How long should I allow?

As an early planning allowance, a modest bathroom wall-and-floor tiling scope might need around 3–7 working days of tiling-related work, with additional time for removal, repairs, waterproofing and curing where required. This is an illustrative range, not a quotation or measured company average. A full bathroom renovation takes longer and requires a separate programme.
