Before you begin

A few good
questions.


Still have something on your mind?

Ask us on WhatsApp 






What areas do you cover?+


CoolInterior serves London and surrounding areas. Include your postcode or area in your enquiry so we can confirm coverage and availability for your project.





Can I enquire about more than one service?+


Yes. Select tiling, marble and interior design in the quote form. We can discuss layouts, colours, surfaces, materials and the order of work together.





What should I include to get a useful quote?+


Tell us the service, location, approximate dimensions and what you would like done. If you have photos, drawings, material choices or a target date, include those too. Attach photos in WhatsApp or email after opening your enquiry.





Can you provide a price from photos?+


Photos and dimensions can help us understand the work, but some projects need a site visit or further discussion. The final scope and price should be agreed before work begins.





Do I need to buy materials first?+


You can enquire whether you already have materials or are still deciding. Tell us what you have purchased, if anything, so we can discuss what else is needed before work starts.





How do I send my quote request?+


Complete the short form and choose WhatsApp or email. Your project brief opens in the selected app. Press send there to deliver it to CoolInterior. You can also call us directly on 07365 155 414.

Privacy, kept simple.


This website builds an enquiry from the details you enter. The form does not save your contact information on a website server.


When you choose WhatsApp or email, your enquiry opens in that service. You must press send there to deliver it. Information you send is handled by that service and by CoolInterior to discuss your project.


Your details are used to respond to your enquiry. Please avoid including sensitive information. Contact booking@coolinterior.co.uk with questions about your information.


The interactive features do not require tracking cookies. Vercel Web Analytics measures page views and visitor totals without tracking cookies. We remove URL query parameters and fragments from Vercel analytics events; form entries are not sent to Vercel Analytics.


With analytics consent, we measure website interactions such as quote starts and phone, WhatsApp or email clicks. These events do not include the contact details or project text entered in the form. A contact click records an action, not a confirmed booking. Cookie preferences are managed through the CookieYes banner.
