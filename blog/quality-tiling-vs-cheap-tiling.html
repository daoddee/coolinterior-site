The true cost of professional tiling in London — and why choosing the cheapest tiler isn't always the smartest investment.

When planning a bathroom renovation, kitchen refurbishment or new tiled floor, one of the first questions homeowners ask is: "How much will the tiling cost?"

It's a reasonable question. Renovation budgets matter, and nobody wants to pay more than necessary.

But there's another question that's arguably even more important:

"Am I paying for a job that will last, or am I paying for something I'll have to replace?"

At Cool Interior, we've worked with homeowners and businesses across London who understand the value of professional workmanship. We've also encountered customers who initially chose a cheaper tiling quotation, only to discover that the lowest price wasn't necessarily the best deal.

The difference between cheap tiling and quality tile installation isn't always immediately visible. Two newly tiled bathrooms might look similar on the first day. However, what's happening beneath those tiles can make all the difference to their long-term performance.

In this guide, we'll explain why professional tiling costs vary, what you're actually paying for, how to identify a reliable tiler in London, and why cutting corners can become an expensive mistake.

1. The Customer Who Chose a Cheaper Tiler — Then Paid Twice

One experience that has stayed with our team at Cool Interior involved customers who contacted us for a tiling quotation.

We discussed the work they wanted completed, assessed the requirements and provided what we believed was a fair quotation reflecting the preparation, materials, time and workmanship involved.

However, the customers had also received a cheaper quotation from another tiler.

Understandably, they wanted to save money.

They compared the two prices and decided to go ahead with the cheaper option.

We respected their decision.

Unfortunately, what initially looked like a saving turned into a much more expensive project.

After the work was completed, the customers were deeply disappointed with the results. The quality of the tiling was not what they had expected, and the finished installation fell short of the standard they wanted for their property.

The money they had saved by choosing the cheaper quotation no longer seemed like a saving.

Eventually, they contacted us again.

This time, however, the situation had changed.

Rather than working with an area ready for the originally proposed installation, we now had to account for removing the unsatisfactory tiling and preparing the surfaces for the replacement work.

The customers ended up paying for three things:

  • The original cheap tiler's work, which they were unhappy with.
  • The additional labour required to remove the poorly finished tiling and prepare the area again.
  • Our original quoted installation price to complete the work to the expected standard.

In other words, they still paid the price we had originally quoted — but only after spending additional money on an installation they were dissatisfied with and the work required to put it right.

The project ultimately cost them considerably more than it would have if they had chosen the right installer from the beginning.

We share this experience not to criticise other tradespeople or suggest that every affordable tiler delivers poor workmanship.

There are excellent tradespeople offering competitive prices.

The lesson is that a cheaper quotation only represents good value when the quality, preparation and installation standards are comparable.

Sometimes, the most expensive tiling mistake is choosing the lowest quote without understanding what it includes.

2. Cheap Tiling vs Quality Tiling: What's the Real Difference?

It's easy to assume that tiling simply involves spreading adhesive, laying tiles and applying grout.

In reality, high-quality tile installation requires much more.

A professional tiler needs to understand surface preparation, tile layout, adhesive selection, waterproofing requirements, movement joints, accurate cutting and finishing details.

These factors influence not only how your tiles look but also how well the installation performs over time.

Surface preparation

Before a single tile is installed, the underlying surface needs to be assessed.

An uneven floor, unstable substrate or poorly prepared wall can compromise the entire installation.

Proper preparation may involve removing existing materials, levelling surfaces, addressing unsuitable backgrounds and ensuring the chosen installation system is appropriate.

A quote that excludes necessary preparation may appear cheaper initially, but the installation could be more vulnerable to problems later.

Tile alignment and precision

Professional tiling involves careful planning to achieve consistent grout lines, accurate levels, balanced tile layouts and clean cuts around fixtures.

This becomes particularly important with large-format porcelain tiles, marble tiles and detailed patterns.

Poor planning may leave awkward cuts, uneven edges and inconsistent spacing that affect the finished appearance.

Correct installation materials

Not every tile or surface requires the same adhesive, grout or preparation method.

The suitability of the materials depends on factors including tile type, substrate, room conditions and installation requirements.

Using unsuitable products or incorrect methods can compromise the work, regardless of how attractive the tiles are.

Waterproofing

Bathroom and shower tiling requires particular attention.

One of the biggest misconceptions in home renovation is that grout and tiles alone make a shower waterproof.

They don't.

Where required, a properly designed waterproofing system must protect the underlying structure.

Getting this wrong can lead to water-related damage and much more extensive remedial work.

Finishing and attention to detail

The difference between average tiling and excellent tiling often appears in the finishing.

Clean silicone joints, consistent spacing, accurately cut edges and carefully planned transitions all contribute to a professional result.

At Cool Interior, we believe the quality of the preparation and workmanship should match the quality of the tiles being installed.

3. Why Is One Tiling Quote More Expensive Than Another?

If you've searched for tilers near me, bathroom tilers London or floor tiling contractors in London, you've probably noticed that prices can vary considerably.

Two tilers may visit the same property and provide different quotations.

That doesn't automatically mean one is overcharging.

The quotations may reflect different assumptions about the work involved.

Some of the main factors influencing tiling costs in London include:

  • Surface condition: Uneven, damaged or unsuitable substrates may require additional preparation.
  • Tile size: Large-format porcelain and natural stone can require specialist handling, precise preparation and more complex cutting.
  • Tile pattern: Herringbone, diagonal and intricate layouts may require additional planning and labour.
  • Existing tile removal: Removing old tiles and adhesive adds labour, waste and possible surface-repair costs.
  • Waterproofing: Bathrooms, showers and wet rooms may require appropriate waterproofing systems.
  • Material selection: Adhesives, grout, trims and other installation materials need to be suitable for the project.
  • Access and logistics: Parking, deliveries, restricted access and site conditions can influence the work involved.

A professional quotation should help you understand these requirements.

The important comparison isn't simply the total price. It's what each contractor is actually proposing to deliver.

A lower quotation may genuinely offer better value. Alternatively, it may exclude essential work that another contractor has already included.

This is why homeowners should compare the scope of work rather than relying on price alone.

4. How Much Can Poor Tiling Really Cost?

To understand the financial risk, consider a hypothetical example.

Imagine you're planning a kitchen floor tiling project in London.

You receive two quotations:

QuotationPrice
Professional tiling quotation£1,500
Cheaper alternative£950
Apparent initial saving£550

At first glance, saving £550 seems attractive.

But suppose the cheaper installation is unsatisfactory and needs to be replaced.

The eventual costs might look something like this:

ExpenseCost
Original cheaper installation£950
Removal and preparation to correct the work£450
Professional replacement installation£1,500
Total spent£2,900

The homeowner who initially wanted to save £550 has now spent £2,900 instead of £1,500.

That's £1,400 more than choosing the professional quotation at the beginning.

And this doesn't include any additional inconvenience, disruption or unexpected damage associated with removing the previous installation.

These figures are illustrative, not actual customer charges or a standard Cool Interior price list. Real costs depend on the scope and condition of the work.

Of course, not every poor installation needs to be completely replaced. Some issues can be corrected through targeted repairs.

Nevertheless, where removal and reinstallation are necessary, the cost of putting things right can significantly exceed the original saving.

5. Five Warning Signs That a Tiling Quote Might Be Too Good to Be True

A competitive price isn't a warning sign by itself. However, homeowners should investigate quotations that appear unusually low without explaining how the work will be completed.

1. The quotation doesn't mention surface preparation

If the existing surface requires preparation, the quotation should explain whether that work is included.

Otherwise, you may face additional charges or an unsuitable installation.

2. The tiler doesn't ask about the tiles

Different tiles have different installation requirements.

A contractor should understand what materials you're planning to use before confirming the scope.

3. There's no discussion of waterproofing

When tiling showers and wet areas, appropriate waterproofing requirements should be considered.

If nobody has assessed how the area will be protected against moisture, ask further questions.

4. The quotation is unclear

A quotation should identify the work being provided and any important exclusions.

You should know whether removal, preparation, installation materials and waste disposal are included.

5. You can't assess previous workmanship

Ask to see examples of similar completed projects.

Look closely at tile alignment, detailed cuts, edges, grout lines and the overall finish.

Testimonials and independent customer reviews can also provide useful insight, although they should be considered alongside the actual scope and quality of work.

A trustworthy tiler should be comfortable explaining what you're paying for and why the work needs to be done in a particular way.

6. Why Quality Tiling Is an Investment in Your Home

Whether you're renovating a family bathroom in Ealing, installing porcelain floor tiles in Hampstead or updating a kitchen in Kensington, good tiling should do more than improve the appearance of the room.

It should provide a durable, practical finish appropriate for everyday use.

Long-term durability

Correct preparation, installation materials and workmanship can help tiled surfaces remain in good condition for many years.

Poor installation, on the other hand, can cause premature problems even when expensive tiles have been used.

Better appearance

Accurate tile layouts, even grout lines, consistent alignment and clean finishing details create a more considered appearance.

This is particularly important for marble, natural stone and large-format porcelain, where installation details can be highly visible.

Reduced risk of unnecessary repairs

No contractor can guarantee that a tiled surface will never need maintenance.

However, a correctly designed and installed tiling system can help reduce avoidable failures resulting from poor workmanship.

Protecting your renovation budget

If you're investing in premium tiles, bathroom fittings or kitchen finishes, saving money on installation at the expense of quality may undermine the wider investment.

Premium materials deserve workmanship that allows them to perform and look their best.

7. Is It Worth Paying More for a Professional Tiler?

Not necessarily more for the sake of paying more.

An expensive quote does not automatically guarantee high-quality work, just as a cheaper quote doesn't automatically mean poor work.

The goal is to find the best value.

For homeowners comparing tiling companies in London, we recommend asking:

  • What surface preparation is included?
  • Are adhesive, grout, trims and other installation materials included?
  • Does the quotation cover removal of existing tiles?
  • What waterproofing provisions are included where necessary?
  • Can I see photographs of comparable projects?
  • What happens if unexpected substrate problems are discovered?
  • What is the anticipated project timeline?
  • Is the work covered by any workmanship guarantee, and what are its terms?

Once you have these answers, you can compare quotations more confidently.

At Cool Interior, our objective is not to be the cheapest tiling company in London.

Our objective is to provide competitive, transparent pricing for quality workmanship, so customers understand the value of the work they're commissioning.

We would rather explain why a project costs what it does than promise an unrealistic price that compromises the finished result.

8. The Hidden Cost of Replacing Poorly Installed Tiles

One point that is frequently overlooked when comparing tiling prices is the cost of undoing previous work.

When a homeowner decides to replace a disappointing installation, the solution isn't always as simple as placing new tiles over the old ones.

Existing tiles may need to be removed.

Adhesive residue may have to be stripped away.

The underlying floor or wall may require repairs.

In bathrooms, the waterproofing system may need to be reassessed.

And because removing tiles can damage the background, additional preparation may become necessary before the new installation can begin.

This is why remedial tiling can sometimes cost more than installing tiles on a properly prepared surface in the first place.

10. How to Get the Best Value From Your Tiling Budget

You don't need an unlimited renovation budget to achieve a high-quality tiled finish.

In fact, careful planning can often help control costs without compromising essential installation standards.

Here are a few ways to make your budget work harder.

Choose tiles suited to your project. Premium marble isn't essential for every room. Quality porcelain and ceramic tiles can offer attractive, practical alternatives depending on the intended use.

Keep layouts practical. Intricate patterns and highly detailed cutting can increase labour requirements. Simpler layouts may reduce installation complexity while still looking elegant.

Understand the condition of existing surfaces. Identifying preparation requirements early can reduce the likelihood of unexpected costs.

Compare like-for-like quotes. Make sure every contractor is pricing the same work, including preparation, installation materials and finishing details.

Avoid false economies. Saving money on tile selection or design complexity may be sensible. Removing essential waterproofing, using unsuitable materials or skipping necessary preparation is not.

The aim should be to spend your renovation budget efficiently, rather than simply choosing the lowest number on paper.

Frequently Asked Questions About Tiling Costs and Quality

Is cheap tiling always poor quality?

No. Some experienced tilers offer excellent workmanship at competitive prices. The concern is when a low quote excludes necessary preparation or doesn't reflect the time and materials needed to complete the work properly.

Why do professional tilers charge more?

A higher quotation may reflect more extensive surface preparation, specialised installation requirements, experienced workmanship, suitable materials and detailed finishing. Always ask what is included rather than assuming a higher price automatically means better quality.

Is it worth paying extra for a good tiler?

It can be. Proper preparation and skilled installation can improve durability and reduce the risk of avoidable failures. The best value comes from comparing workmanship, experience, project scope and price together.

Can badly installed tiles be fixed?

Sometimes. Individual loose, cracked or poorly finished tiles may be repairable. However, widespread installation failures, unstable backgrounds or defective waterproofing may require more extensive replacement work.

How much does it cost to remove and replace floor tiles in London?

The price depends on the size of the area, existing tile condition, adhesive removal, substrate repairs, waste disposal, tile selection and installation complexity. A project-specific quotation is the most reliable way to establish the total cost.

How do I find a reliable tiler in London?

Look for relevant experience, examples of completed projects, clear written quotations, appropriate installation methods and credible customer feedback. Ask questions about preparation, waterproofing and materials before accepting a quotation.

Should I choose the cheapest bathroom tiling quote?

Choose the quotation that offers the best overall value. Compare what's included, the quality of previous work and whether the installation methods are suitable for your bathroom. The lowest quote isn't necessarily the most cost-effective option.
