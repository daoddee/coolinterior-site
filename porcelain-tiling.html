What to consider

Porcelain or ceramic?

Both are tile options, but product suitability depends on the specific tile and intended use. Compare manufacturer specifications, finish, thickness, maintenance and installation requirements.

Large-format planning

Larger tiles need careful planning of access, handling, cuts and the supporting surface. Share the exact dimensions and product information when requesting a quote.

Room suitability

An indoor tile should not be assumed suitable outdoors or for a wet floor. Check the manufacturer’s stated applications and slip guidance for the product you select.

Your questions, answered.

Can you install large-format porcelain tiles?

Yes, we discuss large-format tile installation as part of our porcelain tiling service. Share the tile dimensions, thickness, product information and access details so handling, background preparation and the fitting scope can be assessed.

Does a large tile mean fewer installation costs?

Not necessarily. Fewer joints can be offset by more demanding handling, cutting and background preparation. The layout, size, access and surface condition determine the installation requirements.

Are your project photos confirmed porcelain?

The galleries show the appearance and fitting details of our local projects. We do not label a photographed tile as porcelain or a particular stone unless its product details have been confirmed.

How long will the work take?

Timing depends on the area, preparation, tile format, cuts and product curing requirements. Share the scope so we can discuss a realistic sequence and availability before work starts.

Is porcelain the right choice for your project?

Clear information helps you choose a finish that suits your home. These are starting points for a project conversation; the final specification and schedule follow assessment of your space.

Porcelain versus other ceramic tiles

Porcelain is a ceramic tile with very low water absorption. It is often considered for busy floors and coordinated interiors, but tile specifications still vary. Ceramic wall tiles may offer a suitable finish at a different budget. Choose by intended use, dimensions, finish and care requirements, not the material name alone.

Large-format and marble-effect options

Marble-effect porcelain offers a veined appearance with a different care routine from natural marble. Check repeat patterns and lay out the design before fixing. Large-format porcelain needs suitable handling, access and a carefully prepared background; the format can add installation time even when there are fewer individual tiles.

How long will porcelain last?

A suitable, properly installed and maintained tiled surface can last for decades, but there is no universal lifespan or automatic guarantee. Background movement, impact, product suitability and joint maintenance affect the result. A domestic room’s programme depends on preparation and format; ask us to assess the scope rather than assume porcelain always fits faster.
