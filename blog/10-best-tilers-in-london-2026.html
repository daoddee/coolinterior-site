What Should You Look for When Hiring a Tiler in London?

Choosing the best tiler for your property requires more than comparing prices.

Before making a decision, consider five important factors.

Experience: Ask whether the contractor has completed similar projects using the materials you’ve selected.

Previous work: Review completed installations, particularly close-up photographs showing tile alignment, corners and finishing details.

Surface preparation: Confirm that the quotation covers appropriate preparation and waterproofing where required.

Transparent pricing: Ensure the scope clearly identifies labour, materials, removal, disposal and any additional work.

Customer feedback: Look at recent, independently published reviews and ask for references where appropriate.

The cheapest quotation does not always represent the best long-term value. Poor installation can result in cracked tiles, water damage, uneven surfaces and expensive remedial work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who are the best tilers in London?

London has several established tiling specialists, including London Tiling Group, LKM Stone & Ceramics and London Tiler. CoolInterior is our featured recommendation for customers looking for family trade experience, specialist marble work and residential or commercial tiling services.

The best choice ultimately depends on your location, budget, materials and project requirements.

How much does tiling cost in London?

Tiling costs depend on the size of the area, tile material, installation complexity and condition of the existing surface.

Standard ceramic installations are generally less labour-intensive than intricate mosaics, natural stone or large-format porcelain. Waterproofing, levelling and removing old tiles can also affect the final quotation.

For an accurate price, request a detailed estimate based on your specific project.

Is porcelain better than ceramic for flooring?

Porcelain is generally denser and less water-absorbent than standard ceramic tiles, making it a popular choice for high-traffic floors, bathrooms and outdoor installations when the correct product is selected.

Ceramic tiles can still provide excellent value for many indoor wall and floor applications.

Can I hire tilers for a small bathroom?

Yes. Many professional tiling companies undertake smaller bathroom projects, kitchen splashbacks and individual floor installations.

CoolInterior welcomes enquiries for projects of different sizes.

How do I find reliable tilers near me in London?

Start by checking local project portfolios, relevant experience, customer reviews and written quotations.
