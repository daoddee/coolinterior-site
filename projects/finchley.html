The installation shown

Pale large-format tiling extends across the terrace, integrated seating, raised features and stairs. The photographs record both the overall layout and the stair fitting stage.

Compare the stair-edge alignment, changes of level and the way the tiled surfaces meet raised elements. One photograph shows protected edges; another records installation in progress.
