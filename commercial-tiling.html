Commercial tiling.
Planned around your business.

Floor and wall tiling for workplaces, reception areas, hospitality spaces and shared interiors. Tell Cool Interior how your space is used so we can discuss suitable finishes, access and installation planning.

Discuss your commercial project ↗

Choose for the setting

We discuss foot traffic, cleaning routines, moisture exposure and the supplier’s performance data. Appearance matters alongside the practical requirements of the space.

Plan access and sequencing

Share drawings, floor areas, delivery access, working-hour restrictions and any areas that must remain in use. We can then agree the scope and a realistic programme before work starts.

Keep the brief clear

Your quotation should identify preparation, tile supply, installation, finishing and waste arrangements. Changes and any additional requirements are discussed before proceeding.
