Plan connected rooms before choosing the floor.

When a kitchen floor meets a hallway or living space, the junctions deserve as much thought as the main tiled area. Send photographs of thresholds, door clearances, kitchen plinths and existing floor finishes. Explain whether units remain in place and who will remove or reinstate any fittings.

Share the exact tile dimensions and thickness, any underfloor heating information and a sketch of the rooms. These details help the installer review finished levels, layout and preparation before agreeing quantities. Repeated cracks or movement need assessment before another finish is laid.

  • List the rooms separately, with approximate floor and wall measurements.
  • Confirm whether old tiles, adhesive and waste removal are included.
  • Compare physical tile and grout samples against retained cabinets and worktops.
  • Agree access, protection and when each room can return to normal use.

Questions before you book

Can you tile several rooms as one project?

Tell us which rooms are involved and whether the property will remain occupied. We can discuss the sequence, access and dependencies on other trades before agreeing the work.

Can you give a floor tiling price per square metre?

Area is only one factor. Removal, background repairs, tile format, pattern, cuts and floor transitions also affect the scope. Ask for a quotation that separates inclusions and exclusions.
