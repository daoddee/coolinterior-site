Our story · A family-run business

Built by a father.
Carried forward by his son.

Thirty years of a father’s craftsmanship. A son’s passion for engineering and design. One shared ambition: to make homes better.

Where we started

Our story begins with a father’s 30-year career in the trade: practical skill, care for the details and pride in the work. Those values became the foundation of our family business, and the inspiration for the next generation.

A new chapter

Today, his son is carrying that work forward as a graduate mechanical and design engineer from the University of Nottingham. Cool Interior brings together hands-on experience and an engineering approach to planning, materials and problem-solving, with tiling, marble and natural stone installation at the heart of what we do.

Our mission

Beautiful homes. More thoughtful solutions.

We want to improve the spaces people live in, with considered design, lasting finishes and a more sustainable approach. That means exploring durable materials, planning cuts and quantities to reduce avoidable waste, and helping clients make choices that suit their home, budget and long-term needs.

As a family-run business, our name is personal. We aim to carry forward the care and accountability of our father’s work while bringing fresh ideas to every project.

Materials & surface research

Understand the finish before you choose it.

Suhib’s University of Nottingham research examined the texture of curved metal and polymer surfaces using optical measurements and data analysis. It developed a disciplined approach to comparing materials, checking measurements and understanding why different surfaces behave differently.

For your project, that means asking practical questions: how will the space be used, how much maintenance suits you, and is the proposed finish suitable for wet areas, foot traffic or outdoor exposure? We compare appearance with supplier specifications and the condition of your property to help you make an informed choice.

Project & resource planning

Know the scope, the cost and what happens next.

As your project manager, Suhib coordinates the agreed scope, measurements, material quantities, access, deliveries and installation sequence. We plan the people, equipment and preparation needed before agreeing the programme, including product drying and curing times.

You receive a clear quotation and proposed timetable. If inspection reveals hidden damage or the scope changes, we discuss the effect on cost and timing with you before agreeing additional work. Clear communication keeps you informed throughout the project.
