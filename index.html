Our story · A family-run business

Built by a father.
Carried forward by his son.

Thirty years of a father’s craftsmanship. A son’s passion for engineering and design. One shared ambition: to make homes better.

Where we started

Our story begins with a father’s 30-year career in the trade: practical skill, care for the details and pride in the work. Those values became the foundation of our family business, and the inspiration for the next generation.

A new chapter

Today, his son is carrying that work forward as a graduate mechanical and design engineer from the University of Nottingham. Cool Interior brings together hands-on experience and an engineering approach to planning, materials and problem-solving, with tiling, marble and natural stone installation at the heart of what we do.

Our mission

Beautiful homes. More thoughtful solutions.

We want to improve the spaces people live in, with considered design, lasting finishes and a more sustainable approach. That means exploring durable materials, planning cuts and quantities to reduce avoidable waste, and helping clients make choices that suit their home, budget and long-term needs.

As a family-run business, our name is personal. We aim to carry forward the care and accountability of our father’s work while bringing fresh ideas to every project.

Ask us on WhatsApp 






What areas do you cover?+


CoolInterior serves London and surrounding areas. Include your postcode or area in your enquiry so we can confirm coverage and availability for your project.





Can I enquire about more than one service?+


Yes. Select tiling, marble and natural stone installation in the quote form. We can discuss layouts, colours, surfaces, materials and the order of work together.





What should I include to get a useful quote?+


Tell us the service, location, approximate dimensions and what you would like done. If you have photos, drawings, material choices or a target date, include those too. Attach photos in WhatsApp or email after opening your enquiry.





Can you provide a price from photos?+


Photos and dimensions can help us understand the work, but some projects need a site visit or further discussion. The final scope and price should be agreed before work begins.





Do I need to buy materials first?+


You can enquire whether you already have materials or are still deciding. Tell us what you have purchased, if anything, so we can discuss what else is needed before work starts.





How do I send my quote request?+


Complete the short form and choose WhatsApp or email. Your project brief opens in the selected app. Press send there to deliver it to CoolInterior. You can also call us directly on 07365 155 414.
